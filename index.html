I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science, Peking University (北京大学计算机学院), advised by Prof. Lu Zhang. I obtained my M.S. degree from the School of Software and Microelectronics, Peking University (北京大学软件与微电子学院) in 2022 and my B.S. degree from Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine in 2019.

My research focuses on code intelligence, specifically building smarter code language models by integrating programming language structures, enhancing model understanding of software evolution, and improving dataset quality for code tasks. I have published 20+ papers at top-tier venues including ICSE, ASE, ACL, TSE, and TOSEM (all CCF-A).

I have interned at ByteDance, Kuaishou, and Huawei. If you are interested in collaboration, please feel free to email me at liangqy@pku.edu.cn.
